## Recording notes

- Use a clean browser profile (no extensions visible in the chrome).
- Hide bookmarks bar and avoid showing the username in the avatar (use a fresh test account).
- Record at 1920×1080, mp4 H.264, target under 50 MB so Loom or YouTube unlisted is unconstrained.
- Background music optional; voiceover dry and 2-pass-mixed at −18 LUFS.
